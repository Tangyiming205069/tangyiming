Yiming Tang (唐一茗)

Welcome to my page! I hold a Master’s degree in Computer Science from the University of Southern California. I earned my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Business Information Management at the University of California, Irvine. I also work in the GLAMOR Lab at USC under the guidance of Jesse Thomason, focusing on multimodal social signal prediction. Currently, I am fortunate to collaborate with Yue Zhao in the FORTIS Lab at USC, researching controllable search engine optimization using large language models.

Research Interests

I am passionate about exploring how AI can enhance user experiences, particularly in areas such as recommendation systems, social networks, and human-computer interaction (HCI). I believe that good technology should serve all human beings. My current work focuses on multimodal social signal prediction, LLM application in serach engine, and the detection of addictive content in social media, aiming to create AI technologies that are intuitive, accessible, and impactful for users worldwide.

Projects

  • Modality-agnostic Fusion for Emotion Recognition
    Designed a parameter-efficient shared encoder for emotion recognition that processes features individually for multi-tasking (classification and reconstruction), achieving comparable performance with fewer parameters
  • Prompting with Attention
    Improved zero-shot RoBERTa classification performance by using p-tuning to derive a base continuous prompt, then ensembling over the logits of prompt variations with an attention layer. Uses less parameters compared to fine-tuning.
  • AI Text Detection
    Utilized GPT-2 to generate fake text paired with human-written text, modified the objective function with a triplet loss, and employed RoBERTa to achieve over 90% accuracy in distinguishing between human-written and AI-generated text on story topics.
